
# AFM Platform

An advanced, comprehensive smart Android platform designed by Abu-Fathy Al-Muraisy to provide an integrated ecosystem of technological, educational, commercial, religious, and AI-powered services.

## 🚀 Detailed System Modules & Features

### 1. Comprehensive AI Hub & Assistant Directory
* **AI Chat & Assistants:** Integrated access to top-tier models including ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity for general assistance, trusted research, and file summarization.
* **Creation & Media AI:** Specialized tools for code programming, video generation, digital avatars, presentations, and audio/music production.
* **Design & Editing:** Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Adobe Firefly, Canva AI, and Framer AI for image generation and editing.

### 2. Educational Curricula & Global Libraries
* **Local Curricula:** Specialized portals for official Yemeni curricula in Sana'a and Aden.
* **Specialized Physics:** Dedicated reference portals like the "Al-Fareed in Physics" website.
* **Global Digital Libraries:** Integrated access to Open Library, Internet Archive, Google Books, and Hindawi Books.

### 3. Curricula Solutions & Digital Books
* **Curriculum Search:** Advanced search engines for school curriculum solutions.
* **General Electronic Library:** Comprehensive digital reading rooms.

### 4. Comprehensive Religious Section
* **Quranic Services:** Offline and online text Quran reader and complete MP3 audio Quran streaming.
* **Daily Essentials:** High-accuracy Qibla Finder and Hisn al-Muslim Azkar.
* **Religious Research:** Al-Shamil library, IslamWeb fatwas, and live broadcasting from Makkah.

### 5. Multi-Platform Browser Hub
* Direct secure browsing across major global platforms (Google, YouTube, TikTok, Wikipedia, X, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp).

### 6. Comprehensive Sports Center
* Live match updates, standings via Kooora, FilGoal tracking, and sports TV broadcasting.

### 7. Abu Fathi Studios
* AI music generation (Suno AI, Udio), text processing via ChatGPT, and custom community design requests.

### 8. Osama Educational & School Secretary Center
* Comprehensive grade recording, result processing, student rosters, official forms, and school timetables.

### 9. Global Weather & AI Center
* Real-time meteorological tracking, AI forecasts, cloud radars, and live streaming webcams.

### 10. Technology & Satellite Internet
* Starlink tools, Wi-Fi router guides, Satbeams coverage, and USSD recharge codes for Yemen Mobile, YOU, Sabafon, and Y-Telecom.

### 11. Network Speed Testing
* Built-in tools for network diagnostics via nPerf, Speedtest, and Fast.com.

### 12. Ramadani & Cultural Competitions
* Interactive online cultural quizzes and community competitions.

### 13. Social Apps & Messaging Platform
* Direct web integration for WhatsApp, Telegram, Snapchat, and social channels.

### 14. Live TV Broadcasting Hub
* Multi-channel live streaming including news and sports channels.
