# AFM Platform

An advanced smart Android platform designed to provide diverse technological, educational, and commercial services.

## Key Sections & Features
* **Smart AI Hub (قسم الذكاء الاصطناعي):** Integrated access to advanced AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity & AI Image Generators) for chat, voice, and analysis.
* **Educational Curricula (قسم الكتب والمناهج اليمنية):** Comprehensive libraries covering school and university curricula (Sana'a / Aden), physics, and world languages.
* **Commercial & Local Guide (مجمع مريس التجاري والفندقي):** Showcasing local commercial directories, announcements, and regional services.
* **Religious Services (القسم الديني الشامل):** Essential religious tools, Quranic libraries, and daily services.
